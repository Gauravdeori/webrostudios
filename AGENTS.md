- All editable site copy/data lives in src/data/content.ts; sections in src/components/sections read from it — keeps content edits in one place.
- Layout chrome (navbar, footer, floating buttons, section rail) is mounted in __root.tsx around <Outlet /> — shared on every page.
- Colours are theme tokens in src/styles.css. Use `ink` for text/borders on page surfaces (it flips in dark mode); `navy` stays dark in both themes (dark bands, text on lime).
- Do not edit src/routeTree.gen.ts — it is generated by the TanStack router plugin.
